Welcome to my homepage! My name is Weiqi Meng (Archie Meng). I got the M.S. degree in the [School of Automation at Central South University](https://soa.csu.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Dongran Song](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/songdongran1/zh_CN/index.htm), majoring in Electrical Engineering . Prior to embarking on my postgraduate study, I obtained the B.E. degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from the [Faculty of Electrical Engineering at Kunming University of Science and Technology](https://pwee.kmust.edu.cn/index.htm) in 2021, advised by [Prof. Bo Yang](https://pwee.kmust.edu.cn/info/1036/1143.htm). From May 2026 to August 2028, I worked as a Research Assistant in Electrical Engineering at [Iowa State University](https://www.iastate.edu/).

I am currently pursuing the PhD degree under the supervision of [Prof. Siqi Bu](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/eee/people/academic-staff-and-teaching-staff/prof-bu-siqi/) in the [RISA](https://www.researchgate.net/lab/RISA-PolyU-Siqi-Bu) Research Group at the Hong Kong polytechnic university.
